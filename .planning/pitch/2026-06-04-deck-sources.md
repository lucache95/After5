## ❌ Killed in verification — DO NOT use unqualified

- **Hinge male Gini 0.73 / female 0.63** — refuted (0-3). Only the male **0.542** figure is supportable.
- **Tinder "Gini ~0.58 / bottom 80% of men compete for bottom 22% of women"** (Worst-Online-Dater, 2018) — not independently verified. Do not cite as fact.
- **OkCupid / Rudder "women rate 80% of men below average"** — not independently confirmed.
- **"~7 matches → 1 real date" funnel** — no citable source found. Dropped from the deck.
- **BusinessOfApps benchmarks** (3.3% retention; day-1 25% → day-30 7% funnel; <5% of subscribers active at 12 months) — refuted (0-3 / 1-2). Unreliable.
- **ConsumerEdge experience-economy growth** (experiences +32% vs goods +21%) — refuted (1-2).

## ⚠️ Gaps to fill before pitching (no verified figure exists)

- **Global online-dating market size (USD) + projection** — pull a cited figure from **Statista / Grand View / Mordor** and put the report name + year in the appendix. The deck currently uses verified *spend* and *incumbent revenue* as the market proxy instead.
- **Match-to-date conversion rate** — no citable figure; left out.
- **Reliable churn / 90-day uninstall rate** — BusinessOfApps refuted; needs a primary source if used.

## Time-sensitivity

Match (Q3 2024) and Bumble (Q1 2025) are single-quarter snapshots — refresh from the latest earnings before the deck goes out. Both confirm a downtrend; the magnitude moves quarter to quarter.
