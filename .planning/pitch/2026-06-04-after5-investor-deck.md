## 4 — Insight
**chemistry is discovered in context.** You don't know if someone's for you until you've shared an experience. Every app optimizes the part before the experience: the photo. → **match on the night you'd share, not the face you'd judge.**

## 5 — The script, flipped
1. **effort inverts** — craft a real night, not a "hey." Spam stops working.
2. **judgment inverts** — swipe the experience, not the face. The looks-lottery breaks.
3. **agency inverts** — the host picks first. That choice unlocks the reveal.
4. **pressure inverts** — pass with no awkwardness; the queue promotes the next person.
**social demand:** *"12 people matched this date"* = proof, scarcity, status. Orientation-neutral, works for everyone.

## 6 — Solution: the loop
browse nights → **match** → reveal → plan → date → review. No endless chat. No catfishing. No dead profiles. **The loop closes and never traps you. Every match arrives with a plan attached.**

## 7 — The moat
**AI compute is what makes after5 possible.** Genuinely great dates on demand — real venues, real timing, real photos — at quality, in seconds, for cents. Couldn't be done a few years ago. **Hallucination-proof by design:** the model writes the vibe, never invents a place. The engine is the defensible core, and it travels.

## 8 — Why now
1. **AI crossed the bar** — quality date plans now cost cents; impossible in 2022.
2. **Gen Z is leaving** — 78% burned out, Tinder users down 9% YoY.
3. **Experiences are hot** — spending keeps shifting from stuff to going out.
