## Slide 1 — the problem + the flip

**Headline:** swipe on the date, not the face.

- dating apps are winner-take-all. top 10% of men get 58% of likes. bottom 50% get 4.3%. (Hinge analysis, 2017–18)
- 78% of users are burned out (Forbes Health, 2024). tinder users down 9% YoY. bumble revenue down 7.7% (SEC, 2024–25).
- after5 flips it. swipe a real planned night. match on the experience. identity reveals as it deepens.
- every match arrives with a real date already attached.

## Slide 2 — the product + the moat

**Headline:** built. live. defensible.

- live today (web app).
- an ai date engine generates real, bookable nights. real venues, timing, photos. hallucination-proof.

## Slide 3 — the business + the ask

**Headline:** $500K CAD for 10%. ($5M post-money)

- after5 pro: $19.99 / month subscription.
- self-serve venue marketplace: placement plus booking take-rate.
