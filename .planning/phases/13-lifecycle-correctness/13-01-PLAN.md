<objective>
Fix the LIFE-04 conflict cascade: the `standby_roll` job handler currently ignores `payload.kind` and reads a non-existent `instance_id` key, so `match_auto_roll(null)` silently no-ops and the two cascade consumer RPCs (`match_autoclose_creator_conflicts`, `match_autowithdraw_user_conflicts`) are never invoked. Branch the handler on `payload.kind`, route each kind to its consumer RPC with correctly-keyed args, and leave a Deno regression test that asserts the routing by *effect* (which RPC was called with which args), not by job status.

Purpose: Accepting an offer that conflicts with the host's or candidate's other nights must actually close/withdraw those conflicts — today the machinery is fully inert.
Output: A corrected `standby_roll` handler, a regression test covering all three kinds + the `time_range` round-trip, and a read-only prod verification record.
</objective>
