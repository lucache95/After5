# Reference Itineraries

These are the 10 hand-built reference itineraries that define After5's quality bar. They serve three purposes:

1. **Quality benchmark** — anything the generator produces should feel at least this good.
2. **Concierge content** — post these as Instagram carousels during week 1 (one per day for 10 days).
3. **Test fixtures** — when wiring up the pipeline, these become the "expected output" examples to verify against.

Full text lives in `PLAN.md` Part 6. Quick index:

Mix vibes and seasons so the feed doesn't look monotonous:
day 1 → #2, day 2 → #4, day 3 → #6, day 4 → #1, day 5 → #5,
day 6 → #3, day 7 → #10, day 8 → #7, day 9 → #8, day 10 → #9.
