# Concierge Log

Track every hand-planned itinerary you deliver during the validation phase. The patterns you spot here are the seed data for templates and scoring.

## Patterns to watch for

- Which vibe combos get requested most?
- Which questions do users add unprompted? (those become inputs)
- Which combinations am I instinctively avoiding? (those become negative pairing rules)
- What constraints surprise you? (kid-friendly, dog-friendly, accessibility, dietary)

## Volume target

10 hand-planned dates by end of week 1.
30 by end of week 2.
50 by end of month 1 — that's enough signal to validate.
