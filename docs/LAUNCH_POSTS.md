## TikTok script — "I built an app that plans your Kelowna date in 30 seconds"

**Format**: face-on-camera in the first 1.5 seconds → cut to phone screen → cut to real date footage.

**Length**: 45-60 seconds.

**Script**:

> [On-camera, 0–2s] "I built an app that plans your Kelowna date in 30 seconds."
>
> [Phone screen recording, 2–10s] *Show the 5-step flow happening in real time. Speed it up if needed but no faster than 1.5x.*
>
> [On-camera, 10–14s] "It picked Knox Mountain at sunset, BNA Brewing, then Parlour for ice cream. Fifty bucks per person. Watch."
>
> [B-roll of actually doing the date, 14–50s] *Hike footage at golden hour, lake view, BNA shot of beers, Parlour ice cream shot, walking the boardwalk.*
>
> [On-camera close, 50–60s] "After5 dot app. Free. First plan didn't suck."

**Why this works**:
- Hook in first 2 seconds (the algorithm decides whether to keep showing your video here)
- Shows the actual app working
- Shows the actual date — proof, not a pitch
- Doesn't oversell ("first plan didn't suck" beats "best date of my life")

**Posting cadence**: One TikTok per week for the first month. Each one tests a different vibe ("free Kelowna date", "rainy Kelowna date", "anniversary Kelowna date"). Feed the algorithm category breadth.
